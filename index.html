Featured Capstone Research

Clean Beauty Research

Thesis

“The clean beauty market is growing at a measurable, accelerating rate, and the brands sustaining that growth lead with honest messaging, transparent formulations, and verified safety standards. Marketing that educates builds more durable trust than marketing that simply sells.”

Project components

  • Primary consumer survey · n=97
  • Market size & growth data
  • EWG & Yuka audit: ILIA vs. L’Oréal
  • Greenwashing + MoCRA framework
  • Brilliant brand concept

View the full website here, or check out my data analytics research project on clean beauty marketing and reaching digital audiences.

Research Findings

I surveyed 97 Texas women and audited four matched product categories from L’Oréal Paris and ILIA on EWG Skin Deep scores. The two brands averaged nearly the same, and the clean-positioned one scored worse in half the pairs. The full audit is on the research site.

$35.30Bprojected market size by 2033, up from $10.49B today
78%discovered clean beauty on TikTok or Instagram
57%would switch to a clean hair dye (80% among high spenders)
11 vs 1,400+cosmetic ingredients banned in the US, compared with the EU

One respondent, age 18 to 24: “My mom was diagnosed with cancer and ever since then we have made a switch to clean products.” That is the driver behind the numbers.
